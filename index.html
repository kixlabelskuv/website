This partnership represents a shared focus on creativity, individuality and expressive entertainment.

THE HISTORY OF KIX LABELS

KIX ARCHIVE

A timeline of the people, projects, rebrands and moments that eventually became KIX Labels.

JAN 2024 XM Entertainment is formed.
FEB 2024 Aisha joins XM Entertainment.
OCT 2024 Riki joins XM Entertainment.
OCT 2024 A girl group where Riki and Aisha were supposed to debut fails to launch.
JAN 2025 XM Entertainment is rebranded to Alytrics Entertainment.
JAN 2025 Sui and Hoseok join Alytrics Entertainment.
2025 A boy group and girl group are set to debut in August 2025, but both projects ultimately fail to debut.
JAN 2026 Alytrics Entertainment enters a deep hiatus.
JUL 2026 Alytrics Entertainment fully rebrands into KIX Labels.
JUL 2026 Mae / Miich, CEO of Illusion Entertainment, joins.
JUL 2026 Sophie joins KIX Labels and becomes a member of EUNÖIA.
2026 EUNÖIA officially becomes a KIX Labels group.
2026 KIX Labels × Illusion Entertainment partnership forms.
EARLY AUG 2026 Haeumi auditions for EUNÖIA.
MID AUG 2026 Haeumi officially joins EUNÖIA, But ends up leaving.
30/8/2026 Heaven Joins EUNÖIA to replace Haeumi the same day Haeumi Leaves
